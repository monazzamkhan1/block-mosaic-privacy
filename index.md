# Block Mosaic Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Block Mosaic is an Android puzzle game published by MK Digital Studio PK. It does not require an account. Game progress, settings and scores are stored locally. You can clear this information in Android's app settings or uninstall the game.

The current test release uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK with test ad units. Google may process IP addresses, device identifiers, diagnostics and ad interactions. Where applicable, the app shows Google's consent prompt and provides a Privacy options entry in Settings. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

If you turn on Daily reminders, the app schedules a local notification. You can turn reminders off in Settings. The game is intended for players aged 13 and older.

For privacy questions, contact MK Digital Studio PK through the app support contact on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=pk.blockmosaic.game).


## Google sign-in and cloud saves

In builds that offer Cloud Saves, Google sign-in is optional. Firebase Authentication uses your Google account email and Firebase user ID to identify your Block Mosaic account. If you choose to back up your phone, Cloud Firestore stores your progress, scores, saved games and settings for restoration on another device. Your backup is private to your account.

## Delete your Block Mosaic account

To request deletion of your Block Mosaic account and associated cloud backup without the game, [email MK Digital Studio PK](mailto:monazzamkhan@gmail.com?subject=Block%20Mosaic%20account%20deletion) from the address you used for Google sign-in. Use **Block Mosaic account deletion** as the subject. We may confirm you own the account before processing the request.

If your build has **Delete Block Mosaic account** in **Settings → Cloud Saves**, you can also start deletion there. To remove progress stored on the phone, clear Block Mosaic’s app data in Android settings.
